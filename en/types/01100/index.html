How the Enforcer type is built (01100)

The code lists Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness and Emotional Stability in that order. A score at or above 50% is 1; below 50% is 0. High dimensions: Conscientiousness, Extraversion. Low dimensions: Openness, Agreeableness, Emotional Stability.

Job-group notes for The Enforcer

💰 Finance / Accounting / Legal

“The internal-control lead who puts rules first — gatekeeper who allows no exceptions”

You do not move until the control is written down. Conscientiousness 92% and extraversion 85% make you a visible, exacting owner of internal controls; low openness and agreeableness make you resist exceptions and speak bluntly, and low emotional stability means a control failure feels personal. Controls that nobody feels policed by are the ones that work. Automate enforcement and your team can accept the rules more easily.
