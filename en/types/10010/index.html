How the Dreamer type is built (10010)

The code lists Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness and Emotional Stability in that order. A score at or above 50% is 1; below 50% is 0. High dimensions: Openness, Agreeableness. Low dimensions: Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Emotional Stability.

🔭
OpennessHigh

You are strong at new ideas and creative approaches. You are the first to explore cutting-edge AI tools.

📋
ConscientiousnessLow

Flexible and spontaneous. AI that quickly structures your ideas is your best helper.

🤝
ExtraversionLow

You enjoy deep focus and independent work. Research and analysis AI that raises quality on your own fits you.

💚
AgreeablenessHigh

Empathy and cooperation are your strengths. Team communication and customer-response AI make your relationships shine.

🌊
Emotional StabilityLow

You are sensitive and emotionally aware. Mental-wellness and mindfulness AI help protect your energy.
