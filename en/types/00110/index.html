How the Social Butterfly type is built (00110)

The code lists Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness and Emotional Stability in that order. A score at or above 50% is 1; below 50% is 0. High dimensions: Extraversion, Agreeableness. Low dimensions: Openness, Conscientiousness, Emotional Stability.
