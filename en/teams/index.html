Teams & HR

When a team wants to look at working styles together

If teammates take the same free Big Five (OCEAN) test, you can talk about how people work and where the team may click or clash. Email us with your team size and goal, and we will tell you what we can offer.

What you can ask about

Team personality workshop

After people finish the test, a session to look at types and ways of working together. Tell us the time, group size, and purpose, and we will reply with whether and how we can run it. This page is not a packaged program for sale.

Team composition report

With each person’s consent, we can summarize the mix of types and notes that may help collaboration. We will not collect or share individual scores without consent. This is not a hiring or performance score.

What this test is not. It is a short, roughly 3-minute, 25-question reference tool. It is not a medical or clinical diagnosis, and it is not designed for hiring, selection, or formal performance review. We do not run a certified credential program.

How to start

  1. Email us your team size, purpose, and preferred timing.
  2. We will reply with whether we can help and what the next step would be.
  3. Teammates can take the existing free test on their own devices.
